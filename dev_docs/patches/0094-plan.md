## What was wrong with the draft
The `cart` course added in the earlier commit was never registered, and the course checker rejects it:
- Its Show reused the same screen ids on two screens (ids are shared across a whole demo), and tiles overlapped the cart panel.
- It wrote a gesture as an object (`{ kind: 'swipe', dir: 'left' }`) where the engine wants a string (`swipe-left`), and used a swipe on a screen that did not hold the thing it swiped.
- Show steps tapped and focused things that were hidden at that moment.
- One Info text box was too short for its text, and Info never built anything up (every step had an empty `set`).
- A Guide step pointed at a tile and waited on the till changing without saying its gesture.
- Check 2 injected a problem without saying where it was taught, and its goal ("at least two lines") was already true when the goal began, so it finished instantly.
- Nothing in the Guide taught removing an item, which Check 2 asked for.

## How it works now
- **Info:** builds up one idea at a time (what a cart is, tap to add, quantities, swipe to remove, the total).
- **Show:** one mock screen. Tap the Bee, tap it again (quantity 2), add the Dragon and the Egg, swipe the Egg line left, tap Remove, ready to charge. Matches the real behaviour: a swipe left shows a Remove button (`SwipeRow`, label Remove).
- **Guide:** add the Bee, Dragon and Egg by tapping their tiles, tap the Bee again (quantity 4), then swipe the Red Dragon line left and remove it. On a phone the removal step points at the bar that opens the cart first.
- **Check 1:** build a cart with three **different** items.
- **Check 2:** the cart starts with two Bees and a Dragon, and the customer swaps the Dragon for an Egg. A watch notices if the Bees are taken out too. Taught by the Guide's removal step (`g-remove`).
- Registered after `welcome` and `sign-in`. The remedies table already pointed stray cart mistakes at `cart`, so "taught in Build a cart" now works.
- Removed the claim that swiping right puts a line back (the real row has no such action); the course says to tap the tile again instead.
