# 0100: Training, Find the right item fixes, part 1 of 3

Reported: (1) retry stuck at step 2, (2) the cursor rings the parent, not the button, (3) typing is not checked.

## Causes
1. `Checkout` stays mounted and keeps `tab`, `path`, `variants` in local state. The page chips are hidden while inside a category or the sizes, so step 2's target was off screen and its `recover` said "go to the Checkout tab".
2. Steps pointed at `find.tabs` and `find.keypad` (whole blocks). The native segmented control cannot carry a ref per tab, and Keypad keys had no spots.
3. `g-keys` finished on any key press and `g-type` on any visible Egg result. The repair goal passed for any custom amount.

## Changes
- `src/lib/trainingProbe.ts` (new): Checkout publishes where it is and what is typed; it also registers a reset. `resetCheckout()` runs on sandbox start, goal restart and leave.
- `Step.aim`: per-check answer for what to say and point at now. Used by `gridAim` (back to the grid) and `keysAim` / `wordAim` (typing).
- `Spot.tsx`: slice spots (`registerSlice`, `sliceRect`), so `find.tab:<x>` is an equal part of `find.tabs`. `kit.tsx` Keypad: `find.key:<k>` per key.
- `TrainSnap`: `typed`, `where`; `SnapLine.unitCents`.
- `find-items.ts`: steps and the repair goal use the above.

## Not in this patch
Scroll awareness, brand orange, per-line window context, correction chains and the card-paid-by-cash fix are in 0101 and 0102.
