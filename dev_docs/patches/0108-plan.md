## How it works
- **Course `pay-cash`** (`src/training/modules/pay-cash.ts`, eight minutes, `card: 'auto'`): Info (six steps), Show (ten steps), Guide (fifteen steps), Check 1 (exact cash; $20 for $14) and Check 2 (short and finds more; 5c rounding; a drawer that cannot make change).
- **Spots.** `cash.pad` (the notes and coins), `cash.denom:<cents>` for each note and coin (`cashDenomSpot(2000)` is the $20), `cash.undo`, `cash.take`, `cash.next`, `cash.gave`, `cash.smaller`, `cash.partcash`. `DenomPad` takes an optional `spots` prop, so the Drawer and the other screens that use it are unchanged.
- **What a step can now test.** `cash` (what has been tapped as received, what is due, and which step the window is on) and `lastSaleCash` (what the customer handed over and the change given on the last finished sale). `cash` is published by the Cash window through `trainingProbe.ts`.
- **Check 2 scenarios use the real logic.** The 5c rounding goal is set up with a cart injection of 7% off the Bumble Bee ($7.44, cash due $7.45). The no-change goal uses a `ledger` injection of $50, $20 and $10 notes only, where the real change planner cannot make $6.00 and offers "Cash $10.00 + the rest another way". `tests/trainingpaycash.test.ts` runs both through `priceCart`, `cashDue`, `planChange` and `partCashOption`, so the course can never describe something the app does not do.
- **Part payments.** In "short by $4.00" the tempting "Take $10 as a part payment" button is spotted (a watch with `fix: 'restart'`), because that is a different sale from the one asked for.
- `tests/trainingreceipts.test.ts` now checks that Receipts comes after the card course, not directly after it, because pay-cash sits between them in the plan.

## Not covered
Split payments and part cash with a gift card (their own courses, `split-pay` and `gift-pay`). The "ledger is out of date" button and the smaller-handover option are explained but not forced: the smaller handover needs a drawer layout I could not make the planner offer.
