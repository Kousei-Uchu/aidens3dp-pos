# Patch 0075: going off course in the Checks (new Training list, 3 of 7)

Stacks on 0074.

## What you asked for
- In Check 1 and 2, if you go off course it does not react. It should handle every way you could possibly stray, and guide you back.

## Cause
The driver only kept you on the course's tabs and pages (and quietly pushed you back). It never compared what you had done with what the goal needed, so a wrong item, a stray discount or a finished sale just carried on.

## How it works (`src/lib/trainingCourse.ts`, pure and tested)
When a goal starts, the driver remembers the practice till as it began (the baseline). Each tick it compares the till with that baseline and gives the **first** thing wrong, in this order:
1. **A sale that finished** when the goal did not allow one (cannot be undone, so it offers starting again).
2. **The goal's own `watch` rules** (mistakes the course author knows about, for example taking out the wrong item).
3. **The wrong place**: another tab, another page, or a window that is not wanted (points at the Checkout tab, Back, or the window's Close).
4. **The cart**: something the sale needs was taken out or reduced (points at its grid tile to add it back), something extra was added (points at its line with "Swipe left, then Remove"), too many of one item.
5. **Things added that the goal did not ask for**: a discount, a price change, a customer, a note, or a started payment.

Each goal declares what may change (`allow`, required, can be empty), where it happens (`at`) and its own mistakes (`watch`). Anything else is off course. Removing something the person added themselves is never flagged.

## What the person sees
- A card at the top, "Off course: ...", one plain sentence, with the control to tap **ringed with the pointer** (via the cart bar first on a phone).
- It appears after 0.9 s of the same mistake, so the moment between two taps of a normal path is never flagged. A different mistake starts its own clock.
- **Start this part again** appears when it cannot be undone by hand, or after 12 s of being stuck. A **Start again** button is on the bar for the whole Check. It restores the practice till, drawer and card-decline setup exactly as the goal began, so nobody can get stuck.
- A hint never hides an off-course notice, and the idle "Stuck? Tap Hint" nudge is held back while one shows.
