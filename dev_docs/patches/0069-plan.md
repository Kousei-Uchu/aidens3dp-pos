# Patch 0069: text never cropped in Show and Info (correction 4 of 8)

Stacks on 0068.

## What you asked for
- Sometimes the text is cropped off the screen in Show's animated window.

## Causes found, and fixes
1. **The note was placed against the whole canvas, not what the camera shows.** Zoomed in, the camera sees only part of the canvas, so a note placed below or beside the ringed area could be completely outside the window. Now the note is placed inside the camera's actual view (`viewRect`), trying below, above, right, then left. If no side has room, the camera eases out a little (`settleOn`) until the target and its note both fit. At zoom 1 the whole canvas is visible, so there is always a place for it.
2. **A deep zoom could crop the target itself.** A step's `zoom` was taken literally. The camera now never zooms in so far that the target, with a small margin, would be cut off. A requested zoom is treated as a maximum.
3. **Long labels were clipped inside their boxes** (single line with `numberOfLines`, no way out). A single line now shrinks up to 30 percent to fit instead of being cut, and notes get arrows on any of four sides.
4. **Nothing caught labels that were too long when a course was written.** The checker now estimates each label's width against its box (and wrapping text against its height, including text a step changes) and names the node. It found a real one straight away: the card text in the welcome Info needed about 57 of height in a 48 box. Fixed by shortening the line.

## Tests
- Every case above, plus a test over every real course picture that at every step the target and the note are fully inside the camera's view and the note does not cover the target. 35 training tests pass; `tsc` shows nothing for the files touched.
