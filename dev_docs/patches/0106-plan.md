This patch is 8. Discounts (9) is 0107 and price changes (10) is 0108.

## How it works
- **Course `customer`** (`src/training/modules/customer.ts`, six minutes): Info (six steps), Show (ten steps through list, search, pick, line note, take off, create), Guide (fourteen steps), Check 1 (attach Alex Chen, note the Bumble Bee line, take Alex off) and Check 2 (the wrong customer is on the sale, and a new customer who is not on the list).
- **Spots.** `cart.customer` (the customer row), `customer.search`, `customer.create`, `customer.form`, `customer.save`, `customer.row:<id>` for each customer in the list, and `line.done` (Done in a line window). All the new ones except the cart row are in windows, so steps pointing at them say `sheets: true`.
- **What a step can now test.** `customerId` (who is attached), `customerQuery` (what is typed in the Customer search box) and `noteDraft` (what is typed in a line's Note box). They are read through `trainingProbe.ts`, which the Customer window and the line window publish to. The note only saves on Done, so typing and Done are two Guide steps.
- **Injection.** A `cart` injection can attach a practice customer (`customer: 'train-cu-1'`), so Check 2 can start with the wrong person on the sale.
- **Practice behaviour.** Create customer in practice makes the customer on this iPad only (id `train-cu-new-...`, nothing goes to Shopify), and the Search Shopify button is hidden. Both are inside `isTraining()`.
- `tests/trainingcart.test.ts` now checks only that the first three courses are welcome, sign-in and cart, so adding a course no longer breaks it.

## Not covered
Typing is not checked for its content (any text counts as a note, any name as a new customer). Customers is also reachable from More; that screen has its own course later in the plan (`customers-list`).
