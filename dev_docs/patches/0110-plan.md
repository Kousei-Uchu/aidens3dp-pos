## How it works
- **Course `price-adjust`** (`src/training/modules/price-adjust.ts`, ten minutes, `card: 'auto'`, routes include `pay`): Info (six steps), Show (eleven steps), Guide (twenty-eight steps), Check 1 (item price, line price, whole order price) and Check 2 (a price on the wrong item, a price that must cover every Sticker Pack including later ones, and a flagged price at the review).
- **Spots.** `price.item.keys` and `price.item.set`, `price.line.keys` and `price.line.set`, `price.order.keys` and `price.order.set` (one pair per window, so a step can tell which window is open), `price.reason`, `price.scope.qty` and `price.scope.all`, and `review.continue`, `review.keep`, `review.adjust`, `review.remove`.
- **What a step can now test.** `priceDraft` (what is keyed in an open price window, as `item:600`, `line:2000` or `order:3000`), each line's own item price (`overrideCents`, `overrideAll`) and whole-line price (`adjustCents`), and the whole order price (`orderAdjustCents`). The windows publish the draft through `trainingProbe.ts`, one entry per window so two never overwrite each other.
- **Injection.** A `cart` item can start with an item price (`priceCents`) or a whole-line price that is already stale (`adjust: { finalCents, seenQty }`), which is how the wrong-item and flagged-price problems begin.
- **The Guide removes each price before the next.** A whole order price and line prices are never both on (the app asks before replacing one with the other), so the Guide takes the line price off before setting the order price and never meets that dialog.
- **Tests run the app's own code.** `tests/trainingpriceadjust.test.ts` prices the practice carts with `priceCart`, checks "All in cart" through `setItemPrice`, and checks through `adjustmentRows`, `unresolvedRows`, `keepRow` and `removeRow` that the Check 2 cart really is flagged at the review and that Keep or Remove clears it, and that the Guide's own order is not flagged.

## Not covered
Role limits on who may change prices (Settings), the replace-the-other-price confirmation, and the "Check the order price" flag inside the Whole order price window (the line-level flag is practised at the review). Practice allows every role to adjust.
