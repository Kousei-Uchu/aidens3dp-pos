# Patch 0077: the off-course card stays out of the way (list of 5, point 1b)

Stacks on 0076.

## What you asked for
The "off course" toast sometimes blocks the part of the screen you need. It should know where you need to tap and move itself somewhere around the edges.

## How it works
- `placeNotice` (pure, `src/lib/trainingGeometry.ts`) chooses from six places: top, bottom, and (on a screen wide enough for a narrower card) the four corners. It takes the first place that does not overlap the control to tap (plus 10 units of margin) and the Hint/Exit buttons a window carries. If nothing is clear it takes the least covered place.
- A corner card is narrower, so its height is scaled up for the extra wrapping.
- It keeps its last place while that place is still clear, so a target that moves a little does not make the card hop about.
- `Floating` (in `TrainingOverlay.tsx`) measures the card, asks `placeNotice`, and glides to the new place (about 0.36 s, off with Reduce Motion).
- Used for the off-course card (Guide and Check) and for the Check's goal bar over windows (which prefers the bottom).
- The Guide's own bubble already places itself against its target, so it is unchanged.

## Tests
`tests/trainingnotice.test.ts` (9): default places, target at the top or bottom, never overlapping across a grid of positions on a phone and a tablet, stays on screen, corners on a wide screen, taller corner card, keeps its place, avoids the buttons, least-covered fallback.
