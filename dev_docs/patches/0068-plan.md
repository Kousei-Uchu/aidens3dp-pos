# Patch 0068: sharp Show and Info (correction 3 of 8)

Stacks on 0067.

## What you asked for
- The pixelation on the Show screen is janky. The animation is exactly what you want, so keep it as it is.

## Cause
The picture was drawn once at its normal size, and the camera zoomed in by **stretching that drawn picture up** (up to 2.2 times). A view that has been drawn and is then magnified shows its pixels, so text and edges went soft or blocky exactly when the camera was zoomed in.

## Fix
- The canvas is now laid out at the biggest size it will ever be shown: `renderScale` is the fit of the canvas into the window times the deepest zoom. Every node, font size, border, the ring, the note and the pointer are measured at that scale.
- The camera then only ever **shrinks** the picture: it scales by `zoom / MAX_ZOOM`, which is never above 1. At the deepest zoom the scale is exactly 1, so what you see is drawn natively at full resolution. At lower zooms it is shrunk, which stays crisp.
- The animation itself (camera moves, pointer, ring, notes, fades) is unchanged. The maths is the same, only the unit it is done in.
- Info gets the same fix because it uses the same renderer.

## Steps
1. [x] `renderScale` in `src/lib/trainingDemo.ts`; `DemoView` rewritten to lay out at that scale.
2. [x] New test: layout scale is the fit times the deepest zoom, the camera never scales above 1, and the on-screen scale still equals the zoom asked for. `tsc` shows nothing for the files touched.
3. [ ] You: check it on the iPad at the deepest zoom (the Hint button step in the welcome Show) and at the widest.

## Cost
- At the deepest zoom the picture is laid out about twice as large as it appears at rest. It is a handful of simple views, so this should not be noticeable, but tell me if the animation stutters on an older iPad.
