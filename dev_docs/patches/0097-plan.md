# Patch 0097: Sign in course froze after scanning the pass

Stacks on 0096.

## What you asked
> i get soft locked at guide step 5 for sign in, lock, and switch staff, as clicking doesnt do anything anywhere, and theres no way out other than restarting the app because even the skip and exit buttons are not able to be pressed.

## What I found
Step 5 (`g-keep-cart`) is the first step after the pass is scanned. Scanning ran `onDone` (which closes the Switch staff sheet) and closed the scan window in the same moment. The scan window is a second window on top of the sheet, so two windows dismissed together. On iOS that can leave an invisible window covering the screen. Anything under it, including the training bar's Skip and Exit, cannot be pressed.

## What it does
- `StaffLoginPad` (`src/screens/StaffLogin.tsx`): after a good pass scan it closes the scan window first, then calls `onDone` (which closes the sheet) `SCAN_CLOSE_MS` (0.5 s) later. The wait is cancelled if the pad goes away first. A PIN sign-in, a failed scan and the lock screen behave as before.
- It is the same code the real till uses, so the real Switch staff camera scan gets the same fix.
