# Patch 0074: animated Guide (new Training list, 2 of 7)

Stacks on 0073.

## What you asked for
- Guide is not animated enough. It feels very slow and sharply jumpy between guide steps.

## Causes
1. **Slow:** the driver (`TrainingHost`) checked the app every 300 ms, and a step that finished then needed a second tick just to reset itself before the next step could show. The old highlight sat on screen for up to about a second after you had done the thing.
2. **Jumpy:** the dim, the ring and the bubble were drawn straight at the new position with no motion, and when the next target was not on screen yet (a page still opening) the whole screen flashed fully dark.

## Fix
- **Instant reaction.** A tap on a spot, any change to the app's own state, and a page or tab change now ask for an immediate check (30 ms). The poll is a 120 ms safety net. A new step carries straight on instead of wasting a tick.
- **The spotlight glides** from one target to the next (240 to 520 ms depending on the distance, a snap for a mere re-measure). The first time it appears it closes in from a wider ring.
- **The ring pulses with a soft glow** and follows the glide.
- **A pointer** glides to the target and taps it in a loop, with a ripple (not on "Next" steps, where nothing is tapped).
- **The bubble glides** to its new place, its arrow slides, and its words swap with a short fade and rise.
- **A green burst** with a tick blooms where the last target was each time a step is finished, with a haptic tick.
- **A progress bar** of segments (done, now, to do) sits at the top of the bubble.
- **No dark flash:** if the next target is not on screen for a moment, the last spotlight is held for 700 ms before the screen is dimmed fully.
- Reduce Motion turns the motion off (the spotlight and pointer simply snap). Check mode's hint card swaps with a fade too.

- The timing of the driver and the drawing need a device, see step 3.
