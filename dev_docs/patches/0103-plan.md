# 0103: Training, cart-level corrections and fallbacks

Follows 0102 (which left these two items open).

## Changes
- Spots for the cart menu (`cart.menu`, `cart.menu.discount`, `cart.menu.order`), the Remove buttons in the cart discount and whole order price windows, and the note field in a line window. Chains in `trainingCourse.ts` use them, deepest first.
- `SnapLine.noted`: a note on a line is a stray when the goal does not allow notes.
- `src/training/aims.ts`: `gridAim` moved out of find-items and shared; new `tabsAim`. Used on the cart add and quantity steps and the find items back and tab steps.
- Welcome g-tile and sign-in g-open say how to reach their target (a tap).
