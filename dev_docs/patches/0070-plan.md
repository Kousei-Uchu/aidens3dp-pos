# Patch 0070: the whole Show and Info screen visible at once (correction 5 of 8)

Stacks on 0069.

## What you asked for
- The bottom part of the Show screens is hidden and you have to scroll to see more. You should be able to see the entire screen (for that step) at once.

## Cause
Show and Info sat inside a scrolling page. The picture was sized from the window **width** alone (up to 760 wide, so up to about 475 tall), and then the words, step dots and buttons were stacked underneath, so on most iPads and any landscape window the total was taller than the screen.

## Fix
- Info and Show are no longer in a scrolling page. They use the whole space under the stage bar.
- **The picture fits the room that is left.** `DemoView` now fills whatever space it is given and fits the canvas inside it with `fitBox`: the biggest copy of the same shape that fits, never cropped, centred.
- **The words and buttons are always on screen with it.** `demoLayout` picks the arrangement: on a wide window (landscape) the step title, sentence, dots and Back, Replay and Next sit in a column beside the picture; on a tall window (portrait) they sit underneath. The sentence has its own small scroll area inside its box, only for the rare very long sentence, so the buttons never move off screen.
- Guide, Check, the practice intro and the Done screen are unchanged (they still scroll if they need to).

## Tests
- `fitBox` (width-limited, height-limited, same shape, never bigger than the room, not measured yet) and `demoLayout` (iPad and iPhone, both ways round). 13 demo tests pass; `tsc` shows nothing for the files touched.

2. [x] `Demo` in `TrainingRun.tsx` rearranged (side or stack, no page scroll).
