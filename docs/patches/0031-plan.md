## Things I noticed while reading the code (not changed here)
- **Shared settings are pushed once and never retried** (N29). Saving a bundle deal calls `void pushSharedSettings().catch(() => {})`. If the register is offline then, the deal stays on that register only. The same is true of staff, fees, cash rounding, the PIN requirement and the price adjustment limits. The bundles page says so.
- **Cancelling a part-paid sale does not put money back on a gift card.** The gift card is charged the moment you choose it, and the cancel message tells staff to return cash and gift card payments by hand. The Payment page says the same.
- **The reader messages name screens that do not exist by those names.** They say *Diagnostics ▸ Zeller ▸ Setup* (the screen message) and *More ▸ Support ▸ Zeller* (the notification). The real path is More ▸ Support & diagnostics ▸ **Setup (pair terminal)**. The card page quotes the message and gives the real path.
- **The decline sheet always offers Try card again**, even for codes that say to contact Zeller (`retryable` is worked out in `responseCodes.ts` but nothing reads it).
- **A cancel on the terminal itself shows no message** (the result is `CANCELLED`, so the screen just returns to the payment buttons). That seems right, and the page says it.
- **A deal that saves $0 is skipped silently**, for example a **Set price** at or above what the items cost. The editor does not warn about it. The page lists it in the fault checklist.
