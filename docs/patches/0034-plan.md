## Things I noticed while reading the code (not changed here)
- **Check change's Cart total is what remains on the whole sale**, while the Cash sheet's due is the next payment. After **Split amount** the two differ, and the page says to use **Check a different amount**. Not a fault, but it could confuse; say if you would like Check change to follow the next payment amount instead.
- **Check change never works out a part-cash amount when the customer is short.** The "short" answer suggests card or a part payment but gives no amount. The code has the `part: null` field for it, always null.
- **A stray very large handover** stops Check change after 2,000 parts and may answer "can't make the change". It would need a customer holding dozens of different notes and coins, so it is noted on the page only.
- **Part-cash search in Ask for less uses a 20,000-step search, then falls back to largest-first.** If that also fails the part is skipped. Documented in the limits table.
