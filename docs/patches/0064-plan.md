# Patch 0064: Quick Menu grid fits its pane

## What you asked for
- The tile grid leaves a big gap between its last column and the cart pane. Make the tiles scale up or down, keeping their shape, so the columns fit the width. Under half a tile spare: scale up. Over half: scale down and add a column. Better criteria are welcome.

## Steps
1. [x] Re-fetched the repo (HEAD "Fixed Tab Bar & Worker Migration"). Dropped the 0032 help work.
2. [x] Found that `src/lib/gridLayout.ts` already stretched tiles to fill the pane, but only ever **up** (it always took as many whole columns as fit). With a lot spare, tiles got up to a quarter bigger instead of adding a column.
3. [x] Found a probable cause of a *whole-column* gap: the tile width was `(pane - 16) / columns`, so a row could add up to a hair more than the pane in floating point and wrap its last tile onto the next row. The width is now rounded down to a quarter point so that cannot happen.
4. [x] New rule in `tileColumnsForPane`: compare the stretch needed with the columns that fit against the squash needed with one more column, as ratios, and take the smaller change. That is your half-a-tile rule, made symmetric (it switches at about 0.45 to 0.49 of a tile spare rather than exactly 0.5, because growing by 20% and shrinking by 20% are not equally big changes). Minimum of two columns is kept. The tile height still follows from its fixed shape (0.82 of the width), so the aspect ratio never changes.
5. [x] `tests/gridlayout.test.ts` rewritten: a row never exceeds the pane and wastes under a quarter point per column; your two cases (0.3 spare grows, 0.7 spare adds a column and shrinks); sizes stay within about 0.84x to 1.17x of the target once three or more columns fit; narrow and zero-width panes. 4 of 4 pass.
6. [x] Help: the Tile size paragraph and fault list in `sales/grid` updated; `help/help.json` rebuilt.
7. [ ] You: try it on the iPad in landscape and in Split View, and tell me if the tiles now look too big or too small at any size. The target widths (96, 124, 160) are unchanged.

## Not changed
- The text and icons inside a tile keep their size; only the tile box scales.
