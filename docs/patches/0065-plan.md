## What was wrong with the error (2)
`trainingBlocker` refused to start practice if **any** card attempt anywhere in the history had the status `started` or `unknown`, with no time limit. An attempt is left at `started` whenever the app is closed or loses the iPad mid-payment, and nothing ever clears it, so one such attempt blocked training for good. Now:
- `started` only blocks for 5 minutes after it began (the same window the screensaver and staff lock already use for "a payment is in flight").
- `unknown` only blocks while its sale is still the one in the cart. For any other sale there is nothing you could finish from here.
- A part-paid cart still blocks, as before.
- The messages now say which case it is.

## How Show works now (1)
- **Mock screens in a canvas.** A demo is one or more screens made of simple nodes (panel, bar, text, button, tile, row, toggle, field, chip, icon) placed in a fixed canvas, drawn in the app's own colours so it looks like the till. The welcome course's Show is a mock Checkout with the Guide dock, then a "Course complete" screen.
- **Camera.** It zooms in on the control a step is about (up to 2.2 times, picked automatically to fit unless the step says otherwise), pans, and never slides past the canvas edge.
- **Pointer.** A pointer glides to a control and taps it with a ripple, a small press on the control and a tick.
- **The screen responds.** After the tap, nodes appear, disappear, change text, flip a toggle or take a highlight, and a change of screen is a cross-fade.
- **Spotlight and notes.** The picture dims around the ringed control (same amber ring as Guide), and a short note with an arrow sits beside it.
- **Your pace.** Each step plays once when it opens and then waits. **Next**, **Back** and **Replay** are the only ways it moves. There is no autoplay, no Pause, and no "watch it through first" gate. Going Back or jumping shows the finished picture straight away. If the iPad's Reduce Motion setting is on, the movement is skipped and each step just appears.
- **Order inside a step:** camera to the target, pointer taps, the screen changes, camera settles and the note appears.
- **Authoring.** `validateModule` now checks every Show: ids, screens, nodes that fit the canvas, a `tap` on something that is not on screen at that moment, a `focus` that is not on screen afterwards, missing icons, zoom limits. The old Show format (icons sliding about) is gone. Info keeps its slideshow.
