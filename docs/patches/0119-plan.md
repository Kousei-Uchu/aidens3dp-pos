## Causes found (by reading; no device here, so none reproduced)
- **Two windows changing in the same moment** (0097's cause). The line window hides itself while Discount, Item price or Line price is open, and shows itself again when it closes, so Apply closes one window and opens another in the same instant. The cart menu does the same. This matches the Discounts Check 2 lock, which began right after Apply. Fix: `src/lib/sheetGate.ts`, used by every `Sheet`; a window opens at least 450 ms after the last one began to close, and at once if none did.
- **A full-screen dim with no hole.** In Guide, when the target was gone (a window had closed), the overlay dimmed and blocked the whole screen, Exit included (Price step 19, the scan Guide). It now dims only when there is a target to point at.
- **Pointing at something behind a window.** A fallback could ring a page spot that sat under an open window, leaving only that hidden spot tappable (the scan Guide's "tiny gap"). Fallbacks now skip page spots while a window is open.
- **Next blocks touches in the ring.** A step that waits on Next put an invisible blocker over the area, so the Customers form and the Reason box could not be typed in. New `Step.typing` lets touches through and draws no cursor.
- **Deals $0.** The goal finished on the Continue press, but Continue pushes Payment 250 ms later, after the finish screen had opened and the cart was cleared. The goal now ends when Payment is open, and the push is skipped if practice ended.

## Also
- `Step.ready`: Next is greyed out until it is true. Customers uses it (first name plus a real email or a mobile). The form's typed values reach the guide through a new probe (`customerDraft`).
- Customers note step needs Gift wrap typed (any case, any spacing). Its form step now says to type your own details and that nothing is kept.
- Cursor: the ring's orange, 80% opaque, a dark edge, never takes a touch (it already ignored touches), not drawn on Next or typing steps.
- Scan window: compact cards, the Guide's cards first, and a result box that is always there.
- Price step 19 falls back to Line price, then the cart line.
