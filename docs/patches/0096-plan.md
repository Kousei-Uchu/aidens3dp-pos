# Patch 0096: Virtual terminal screens redone from your photos

Stacks on 0095. Seven small commits, so each screen can be reviewed or dropped on its own.

## What you asked
> fix all this stuff. in individual patches. attached are the zeller screens to design the others based on, on ours, all screens be dark mode, and the action buttons and menu in general wont be there. the last image with the logo, is the screensaver logo, and the background is the screensaver background colour. re-do all the virtual terminal screens.

## What it does
1. **Dark mode only.** `faceColors` always gives the dark palette, new terminals and previews start dark, and the Dark screen switch on the Virtual terminal page is removed. The `dark` flag stays on the state so demo files that set it still load.
2. **Idle.** The screensaver logo on the screensaver colour (as before) with the small zeller wordmark at the foot, like your photo.
3. **Tap Now.** Blue panel with the contactless symbol and "Tap Now" on top, the amount on a dark sheet that overlaps it, and a small Cancel link (a real spot, so training can still point at it).
4. **Processing.** The blue smile on the dark screen with "Processing". The hint line is gone.
5. **Approved.** Dark screen, green ring and tick, "$X Approved". No Note, Contact, Receipt or New Sale.
6. **Declined.** Red ring and cross, "$X Declined", a bold "Insufficient funds (51)" line and the explanation. No eReceipt button.
7. **The rest.** Receipt (QR on a white card), Cancelled and Not available use the same outlined ring and dark look.

## Not done
- Receipt options, PIN pad, remove card, cancelled and not available have no photos, so they follow the look of the others but are not copied from anything.
- The smile does not animate.
- `tests/virtualterminal.test.ts` `DWELL_MS` still fails (the Not available screen has no automatic wait); it fails without this patch too.
