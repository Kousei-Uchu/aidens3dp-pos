# Patch 0091: Swap my cash (device test T6, part 2)

Stacks on 0090.

## What you asked (T6)
> I'd like to be able to enter the cash I have personally and see what it suggests I exchange, like if I enter that I have a $50 note from my own pocket and it really wants $50s and we have an excessive number of 5s, it might suggest I exchange my 50 for 10x $5.

## How it works
- More ▸ Cash drawer ▸ **Swap my cash**: tap what you hold (the usual tap pad). It shows **Put in the drawer (yours)** and **Take out of the drawer**, both in squares, an even value both ways.
- `planSwap` (`src/lib/cashSwap.ts`) plays the 0090 imagined day against the drawer before and after each candidate swap. Score = failure points + 0.2 per note or coin held afterwards + 0.05 per note or coin that changes hands. Best swap first, then again with what you still hold, up to six steps, stops when nothing gains 0.4 points. Never takes out the kind it hands in; never takes what the drawer does not hold; steps that cancel are merged.
- Honest note on your example: the simulation does not see a $50 as useful change (nobody needs change over $50), so it will not *want* a $50. What it does see is that ten spare $5s are clutter, so $50 in, ten $5s out comes out when that does not make exact change harder. A drawer that cannot spare anything useful, or already copes, gets *Nothing worth swapping*.
- **I swapped it: record it** writes one Contents corrected entry (net notes in and out). The total is unchanged, so the float and Z-report do not move. Closing the sheet records nothing.
