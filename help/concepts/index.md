## Deep

### Local first, then shared
The register keeps working data on this iPad and shares selected records with Shopify. A completed sale is saved locally before its Shopify upload finishes. Staff, some settings, catalogue and stock are shared or refreshed separately, so two screens can briefly show different update times.
